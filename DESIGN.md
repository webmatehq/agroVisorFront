# Design System: SurcoVisor

<!-- impeccable:design-map -->
Name: SurcoVisor
Colors: primary: ink #113e32; secondary: ref #1d7f4c; tertiary: crop #52606d; neutral: paper #fbfcfa; error: danger #9c2838; data: ndvi-low #a4143c; primary: ink-hover #0b2c23 (+22 more in the sidecar)
Fonts: Hanken Grotesk (display, display-mobile, stat, headline, headline-auth, headline-hub, numeral-lg, title-xl, title-app, title-lg, title, numeral, title-md, title-sm, title-xs, lede, body-strong, body-lg, body, body-sm, ui, caption, label, label-sm, label-micro)
Spacing: xs 8px, sm 14px, md 20px, lg 28px, xl 40px, 2xl 48px, gutter 32px, gutter-mobile 20px, section 136px, section-mobile 96px, page 1280px
Radius: nav 8px, control 10px, inset 12px, panel 14px, card 16px, window 18px, tile 20px, door 24px, pill 999px
North Star: "The Clear Field"
Rules:
- The Data-Only Multicolour Rule. Every colour outside the greens, slate, neutrals and danger encodes data: the NDVI ramp or a chart series.
- The Example Label Rule. Any surface showing fabricated or sample data carries a Crop Slate "Datos de ejemplo" pill.
- The Leaf Is Light Rule. Fresh Leaf is a glow, not a colour you read.
- The Tight Display Rule. Anything 26px and up is 750 weight with negative tracking (-0.02em at 26px down to -0.045em at the hero).
- (+5 more)
Elevation:
- Resting: 0 1px 2px rgba(13, 31, 25, .06), 0 2px 8px rgba(13, 31, 25, .04)
- Lifted: 0 24px 60px -24px rgba(13, 31, 25, .28), 0 8px 24px -12px rgba(13, 31, 25, .12)
- Window halo: 0 0 0 6px rgba(255, 255, 255, .6)
- Primary glow: 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.15) inset, 0 6px 16px -6px rgba(17,62,50,.55)
- Focus halo: 0 0 0 3px #e4f6eb
Don't:
- Don't dress the site as a themed document: no flight-chart, cadastral-plan or survey-sheet language, linework, hatching or monospace lettering.
- Don't use colour outside the greens, slate and neutrals except to encode data (the NDVI ramp, chart series).
- Don't set Fresh Leaf (#3ccf7a) as text or a fill on light ground; it is the hero glow only.
- Don't put uppercase tracked eyebrows or kickers above headings; uppercase labels name fields and stats only.
- Don't use square corners or hard unblurred offset shadows on any surface.
- Don't fabricate testimonials, client logos, pricing or press to fill a section.
