# Design System: SurcoVisor

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Clear Field"**

SurcoVisor looks like a modern software product, not a themed document. The page is near-white and quiet, the type is one confident grotesque, and the real product and real fieldwork carry the story: a working app window on a real NDVI parcel, live demos of indices, weather and the field notebook, and full-bleed drone footage. Craft is measured against Linear and Stripe: tight display tracking, generous whitespace, hairline borders, soft long shadows, pill buttons, and motion that settles rather than performs.

Density is calm on the landing and practical in the app. Brand green is the identity and the primary action; a near-black heading green carries the second action and the headings; one dark green field per view (the drone door, the app sidebar) gives the page weight. Colour beyond the greens appears only to encode data: the NDVI ramp and the weather chart series. Example data is always labelled as such, in a quiet slate.

The landing (Persuade) and the app, module hub and auth (Operate) share one token set in `theme.css`. This system replaced two rejected themed worlds; it keeps none of their language.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-white ground, white surfaces, hairline borders, soft long shadows.
- Hanken Grotesk for every word, display set tight (-0.035em to -0.045em).
- Pill buttons in pairs of equal weight: brand green and near-black.
- Real product UI and real field media as the hero, never illustration.
- Concentric rounded corners, larger on outer containers.
- One rise-and-settle entrance; hover lifts of a few pixels; nothing loops except field video.

## Colors

A green-on-white product palette: one brand green, one accessible link green, a near-black heading green, and a slate reserved for example data.

### Primary
- **Field Green** (ink): the identity colour. Primary buttons, the app sidebar, the drone door's dark field, the brand mark, body text on the app shell and the large story figures. Hover deepens to **Night Field** (ink-hover).
- **Heading Green-Black** (heading): every heading on the landing, the dark pill button (the drone action), strong inline values.

### Secondary
- **Leaf Link Green** (ref): links, focus rings, selection, active nav icons, checklist ticks, positive deltas, pill text. Passes 4.5:1 on paper. **Deep Leaf** (ref-deep) is its hover; **Mint** (ref-light) is the same accent on dark green; **Leaf Wash** (ref-tint) fills icon tiles, pills, focus halos and the auth glow.
- **Fresh Leaf** (leaf): atmosphere only. It appears as the 16%-alpha radial glow behind the hero and nowhere as text or fill on light ground.

### Tertiary
- **Crop Slate** (crop): example data and information. The "Datos de ejemplo" tags, notices, auth secondary links, forecast period and rain values. **Crop Deep** (crop-deep) is its hover; **Slate Wash** (crop-tint) its fill.

### Neutral
- **Paper** (paper): page ground, app ground, product-window chrome bar.
- **Soft Paper** (paper-2): hover fills, ghost-button hover, table heads, segmented-control track.
- **Surface White** (surface): cards, tiles, windows, inputs, footer.
- **Body Ink** (ink-2): running text, ≥7:1 on paper.
- **Quiet Ink** (ink-3): ledes, section intros, secondary text and labels, ≥4.5:1.
- **Hairline** (rule): the 1px border on every card, tile, window and section divider. **Line** and **Line Strong** are the translucent dividers and control borders.
- **Mist on Green** (on-ink-2): secondary text on the dark green field.
- **Danger Red** (danger) with **Danger Wash** (danger-tint): errors only.

### Data
- **NDVI ramp** (ndvi-low → ndvi-high): red, amber, lime, teal, deep green. The index legend, the index icon and the real renders. Never decoration.
- **Temperature Line** (chart-temperature) and **Rain Bar** (chart-rain): the two series of the weather chart.

### Named Rules
**The Data-Only Multicolour Rule.** Every colour outside the greens, slate, neutrals and danger encodes data: the NDVI ramp or a chart series. A multicolour gradient used as decoration is off-system.

**The Example Label Rule.** Any surface showing fabricated or sample data carries a Crop Slate "Datos de ejemplo" pill. Slate never marks an action or an owner.

**The Leaf Is Light Rule.** Fresh Leaf is a glow, not a colour you read. Links and active states use Leaf Link Green.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Hanken Grotesk (variable 100–900, self-hosted woff2, latin + latin-ext; fallback ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Hanken Grotesk
**Label/Mono Font:** none; numbers use tabular figures of the same family.

**Character:** One humanist grotesque at many weights. Headings sit at 750 with tight negative tracking for a confident, product-site voice; reading text sits at 400 with open line height; UI text at 650. Intermediate variable weights (550, 650, 750) are part of the system.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (750, clamp(46px, 6.4vw, 88px), 0.98, -0.045em): the hero headline only. 42px on phones.
- **Stat** (750, clamp(44px, 5vw, 68px), 1, -0.04em, tabular): the story figures (+7 años, +7.000 ha), in Field Green.
- **Headline** (750, clamp(32px, 3.8vw, 52px), 1.04, -0.035em): section titles. The module hub (clamp(36px, 4vw, 52px)) and auth (800, clamp(36px, 4.4vw, 56px)) use sibling steps.
- **Title** (750, 32 / 30 / 28 / 26px, 1.2, -0.02 to -0.025em): door headings 32, app page titles 30, place names 28, tile and contact-card headings 26.
- **Small title** (700–800, 24 / 22 / 21 / 20px, -0.01 to -0.02em): phone numbers and demo metrics 24, auth form heading 22, trio headings and the story lead 21, brand wordmark and module-card headings 20.
- **Lede** (400, clamp(17px, 1.6vw, 20px), 1.55, max 60ch): hero subcopy, in Quiet Ink.
- **Body** (400, 17 / 16 / 15px, 1.5–1.7): section intros and story 17 (max 52–62ch), tile and card copy 16, the base and lists 15. 18px at 800 marks a strong caption in demos.
- **UI** (650, 14px): small buttons, hints, channel labels. Large buttons step to 16px.
- **Caption / Label** (600–650, 13 / 12 / 11px): captions and footer labels 13, pills 12, example tags and stat labels 11.
- **Micro label** (700, 10px, 0.08em, uppercase): field labels inside the miniature product window only.

### Named Rules
**The Tight Display Rule.** Anything 26px and up is 750 weight with negative tracking (-0.02em at 26px down to -0.045em at the hero). Big type at default tracking reads unfinished.

**The Labels Label Data Rule.** Uppercase tracked text (0.08em) names a field, a column or a stat: form labels, table heads, stat labels, the window's context labels. It never sits above a heading as an eyebrow or kicker.

**The Tabular Numbers Rule.** Figures that compare or update (stats, phone numbers, metrics, tables, times) use tabular numerals.

## Layout

A centred 1280px page with a 32px gutter (20px under 820px). Sections are separated by 136px of open space (96px on phones), not by rules or colour bands. Every section opens with a two-column head: headline on a 7fr track, intro on a 5fr track, bottom-aligned, 48px below; it stacks under 1024px.

The hero is centred: headline, lede, two equal 54px buttons (min-width 260px, full width on phones), a small note, then the product window 64px below, up to 1120px wide, bleeding past the fold. Content grids use 20px gaps for card groups (bento, doors, contact, gallery) and 40px gaps for open, border-topped columns (feature trio, places). The bento is two columns with a full-width first tile (4fr copy / 7fr demo). Two doors sit side by side at 560px minimum height. The footer is a 2fr/1fr/1fr/1fr band on white.

Breakpoints as built: 1180px (floating cards tuck inside the window), 1024px (nav links hide, heads, bento, doors and story stack, window sidebar hides), 820px (gutter shrinks), 720px (phone layout: single-column trio and places, two-up gallery, floating cards become static), with 1100px and 700px for the module hub grid.

## Elevation & Depth

A hybrid of hairlines and soft long shadows. Every container has a 1px Hairline border; resting cards add a barely-there two-layer shadow, and only things meant to feel lifted (the product window, floating cards, the platform door's screenshot, hovered module cards, the auth card) get the long shadow. Depth also comes from layering: white surfaces on paper, a paper inset inside a white tile, the dark green field among light ones, and a blurred translucent nav.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Resting** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(13, 31, 25, .06), 0 2px 8px rgba(13, 31, 25, .04)`): tiles, contact cards, app cards, map captions.
- **Lifted** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 60px -24px rgba(13, 31, 25, .28), 0 8px 24px -12px rgba(13, 31, 25, .12)`): product window, floating cards, door screenshot, auth card, module-card hover.
- **Window halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 6px rgba(255, 255, 255, .6)`): a soft white ring added around the product window.
- **Primary glow** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.15) inset, 0 6px 16px -6px rgba(17,62,50,.55)`): primary pill buttons only.
- **Focus halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px #e4f6eb` with a Leaf Link Green border): focused inputs.

### Named Rules
**The Lift Means Product Rule.** The long shadow belongs to product pieces that float (window, floating cards, screenshots) and to hover feedback. Resting content cards stay on the resting shadow.

**The Soft Shadow Rule.** Shadows are diffuse, tinted with Heading Green-Black, and offset only downward. No hard, unblurred offset shadows.

## Shapes

Rounded, concentric and soft. Corners grow with the container: 8px for nav items inside the window, 10px for controls and inputs, 12px for icon tiles and inset panels, 14px for demo panels, floating cards and screenshots, 16px for app cards and the auth card, 18px for the product window and gallery media, 20px for tiles and contact cards, 24px for the doors. Buttons, pills, tags, nav links and badges are full pills (999px); dots, avatars and round icon buttons are circles. Borders are always 1px hairlines on the landing (1.5px on app controls). Checklist items use a round Leaf Wash tick.

**The Concentric Corner Rule.** A nested surface uses a smaller radius than its container; an outer container never has tighter corners than what it holds.

## Components

### Buttons
Confident, round and quiet: pills that press in.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 1px border, weight 650, slight negative tracking.
- **Sizes:** small 38px (14px text), default 44px (15px), large 54px (16px).
- **Primary:** Field Green with white text and the primary glow; hover Night Field. The platform action.
- **Dark:** Heading Green-Black with white text. The drone-service action, always paired with a primary of equal size.
- **Ghost:** transparent with a Line Strong border and heading text; hover Soft Paper. "Entrar" in the nav.
- **Light:** white with heading text, for use on the dark green field.
- **States:** colour transitions 0.2s; press scales to 0.97 over 0.16s with the ease-out curve; focus shows the 2px Leaf Link Green outline at 3px offset. App buttons share the colours at a 10px radius.

### Chips
- **Pill:** Leaf Wash with Leaf Link Green text, 12px/650, names a door or contact card's audience. On dark green it turns white text on 16% white.
- **Example tag:** Slate Wash with Crop Slate text, 11px/650, pinned top-right of every demo panel (bottom-right on the wide tile) and in the window's chrome bar.
- **Media badge:** 92% white pill with heading text and an icon, bottom-left on gallery media ("Ver vídeo").

### Cards / Containers
- **Tile and contact card:** white, 1px Hairline, 20px corners, resting shadow, 32–36px padding (24px/20px on phones). Copy block of icon tile, 26px title, 16px body, check list.
- **Demo panel:** inside a tile; paper fill, 14px corners, 22px padding; the wide tile's demo is white and edge-to-edge.
- **Icon tile:** 44px square, 12px corners, Leaf Wash fill, Leaf Link Green line icon.
- **Open column:** no box; a 1px top Hairline and 28px top padding (feature trio, places).
- **App card:** white, Hairline, 16px corners, resting shadow; module cards lift 2px with the long shadow on hover.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white, 1.5px Line Strong border, 10px corners, 46px tall on auth with 16px text.
- **Focus:** border turns Leaf Link Green with a 3px Leaf Wash halo; no outline.
- **Labels:** 12px/700 uppercase at 0.08em in Body Ink.
- **Error:** Danger Wash fill, 1.5px Danger Red border, 10px corners.

### Navigation
- **Landing nav:** sticky, 68px, paper at 82% with a saturate-and-14px blur backdrop and a Line hairline below. Brand mark and 20px wordmark left, centred pill links (15px/500, hover Soft Paper fill), ghost "Entrar" and small primary "Crear cuenta" right. Under 1024px the links hide; under 720px only "Entrar" remains.
- **App sidebar:** the Field Green dark field; nav items in Mist on Green, hover a faint white wash, active item inverts to paper with a Leaf Link Green icon. The top bar is 92% white with a blur and a Hairline.

### Product Window (signature)
A faithful miniature of the real app: a white window with 18px corners, the lifted shadow and a 6px white halo; a paper chrome bar with three dots, the URL and an example tag; a 200px Field Green sidebar; a paper main area with finca/parcela context, the real NDVI render (12px corners, caption pill with the NDVI ramp) and three stat cards. Two floating cards (weather, notebook) overhang the window edges at 14px corners with the lifted shadow. On load the window rises 28px and settles from 98.5% scale over 0.9s on the ease-out curve (0.1s delay); the floating cards follow at 0.3s and 0.38s. Under reduced motion it is static. Under 1024px the sidebar hides; on phones the floating cards stack below.

### Doors (signature)
Two equal cards, 24px corners, 560px minimum height. The platform door is a white-to-Soft-Paper gradient with a cropped product screenshot rising from its bottom edge (lifts 6px on hover over 0.5s). The drone door is the Field Green dark field with field video at 38% opacity under a green gradient scrim, white heading, mint ticks and a light button.

### Story and Gallery
Two columns: headline with Stat figures left, story copy right (first paragraph 21px/550 in heading colour). Below, a 1.15fr/1fr/1fr gallery of 4:5 media at 18px corners; images and looping muted videos zoom to 1.03 over 0.6s on hover and open in a dialog.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** let the real product (the app window, live demos, real NDVI renders) and real field media (drone photo and videos) lead every section.
- **Do** pair the two actions as equal pills: Field Green primary for the platform, Heading Green-Black dark for drones, same size, side by side.
- **Do** label every sample-data surface with the Crop Slate "Datos de ejemplo" tag.
- **Do** set headings at 750 with negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.045em) and keep reading text at 400 with 1.5–1.7 line height.
- **Do** give every container a 1px Hairline (#dfe5e1) and a radius from the concentric ladder (10 / 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 20 / 24px), pills at 999px.
- **Do** keep motion to one load entrance (rise and settle, 0.9s, cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1)), press scale 0.97, and hover lifts of 2–6px, all disabled under reduced motion.
- **Do** separate sections with 136px of space (96px on phones) rather than bands or rules.

### Don't:
- **Don't** dress the site as a themed document: no flight-chart, cadastral-plan or survey-sheet language, linework, hatching or monospace lettering.
- **Don't** use colour outside the greens, slate and neutrals except to encode data (the NDVI ramp, chart series).
- **Don't** set Fresh Leaf (#3ccf7a) as text or a fill on light ground; it is the hero glow only.
- **Don't** put uppercase tracked eyebrows or kickers above headings; uppercase labels name fields and stats only.
- **Don't** use square corners or hard unblurred offset shadows on any surface.
- **Don't** fabricate testimonials, client logos, pricing or press to fill a section.
