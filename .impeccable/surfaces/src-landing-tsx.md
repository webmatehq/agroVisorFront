# Surface: public landing + auth (src/Landing.tsx, Auth in src/main.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: small/medium producers, agronomists, drone-service clients (ES/LatAm). Two equal actions: open a platform account; request a drone job (WhatsApp/phone). Proof: real NDVI/index renders, real drone photo and videos, +7 años / +7.000 ha. Constraints: keep SurcoVisor + ◈, brand green #113e32, current copy and facts.
